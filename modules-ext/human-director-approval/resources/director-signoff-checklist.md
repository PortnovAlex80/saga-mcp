# Director Sign-off Checklist

> Pinned resource `human-director.signoff-checklist` for the `director-signoff`
> Human node of `human-director-approval@1.0.0` (W10-A3).
> Loaded by NodeProtocol step `present-scoring`. Tick every item before
> recording a decision.

- [ ] **Bundle cited.** I can state the exact `campaignBundleId` under review.
- [ ] **Scoring reviewed.** I have read the scoring summary, not just its
      headline number.
- [ ] **Risk accepted or named.** If approving, I can name the residual risk I
      am accepting. If rejecting, I can name the reason.
- [ ] **Decision is binary.** My decision is exactly `approved` or `rejected`
      (no third option, no conditional approval).
- [ ] **Authority.** I am the director of record (`decidedBy` is my identity).
- [ ] **No synthesis.** I am recording my own decision, not one I inferred the
      runtime or scoring wanted.
