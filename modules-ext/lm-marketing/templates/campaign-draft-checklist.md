# Campaign Draft Completeness Checklist

> Pinned by the `marketing-author` execution profile of `lm-marketing@1.0.0`.
> The worker must tick every item before submitting the `CampaignDraft` bundle.

- [ ] Audience stated and matches the brief
- [ ] Goal stated and matches the brief
- [ ] Every channel is in the brief's allowed channel set
- [ ] Key message reproduced verbatim from the brief
- [ ] Constraints acknowledged (word/length limits, legal, brand)
- [ ] Subject / headline present
- [ ] Body present
- [ ] Channel plan present
- [ ] Call to action present
- [ ] No placeholder `{{...}}` left unfilled
- [ ] No content invented beyond the brief (clarification-required surfaced
      instead, where the brief is internally inconsistent)
